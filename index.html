Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the NSS College Ottapalam App collects, uses, stores and protects information provided by students, teachers and other authorised users.

By using the application, users acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Institution: NSS College Ottapalam
Application: NSS College Ottapalam App
Last Updated: 02 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services used in the application, we may collect information including:

  • Student full name
  • University Register Number
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Department and programme
  • Semester and year of study
  • Course registration information
  • Attendance information
  • Academic and timetable-related information
  • Account and authentication information
  • Requests, complaints and feedback submitted through the app

2. How We Use Information

The collected information may be used to:

  • Provide access to authorised application features.
  • Maintain student and academic records used by the application.
  • Manage attendance and course information.
  • Display personalised academic information.
  • Provide notices, circulars and institutional updates.
  • Respond to requests, complaints and feedback.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application.

3. Data Sharing

Personal information is intended for authorised institutional and application-related purposes. Information may also be processed by service providers used to operate the application where necessary.

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation or a lawful request from an authorised authority.

4. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no internet-connected information system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, institutional requirements, academic record requirements, security, or applicable legal obligations.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users may submit an account deletion request using the form provided on this page.

The institution may verify the identity of the requester before processing a deletion request. Certain information may need to be retained where required for legitimate academic, administrative, security or legal record-keeping purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party infrastructure or services for functions such as database storage, file storage, authentication, notifications and application hosting.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, information practices or applicable requirements change.

9. Contact

Questions regarding privacy or deletion of information may be submitted to the institution or through the account deletion form below.

Request Account Deletion

Students who wish to request deletion of their app account and associated personal information can submit the following form.

Your request may be verified before deletion is processed. Submission of this form does not automatically delete academic records that the institution is required to retain.

For questions regarding your account, personal information or an account deletion request, please contact the authorised college/app administrator.
